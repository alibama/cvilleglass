Why we do this

Everything breaks, especially glass.

Our work is about how we react to unexpected change and loss — finding the best pivot, the deepest acceptance, the honest acknowledgment that sometimes things just aren’t going to work the way you wanted. And then the joy of making something beautiful, having worked through every challenge to get there. In the hot shop we explore all of this, every day. Join us.

What we do

Three ways in.

We guide you through the whole process — as teachers, as training wheels, and, when you’re ready to work on your own, as a cheering section of creative peers.

Studio time for individuals

Guided time at the bench and the kiln, one-on-one or in small groups. We handle the fire while you focus on the making — come as you are, no experience needed.

Groups & partners

Shared, hands-on sessions for teams, schools, recovery and care programs, and workplaces that want a reset built around communication and trust.

Community education

Classes, demonstrations, and open days that make glassblowing, flamework, and kiln forming approachable for the whole community.

What glass builds

The material does part of the work.

Glassblowing can be therapeutic. Our classes give you the skills to use this creative art however works for you — making art, making craft, connecting with a loved one or a co-worker, or rebuilding your sense of self after a difficult experience.

✳

Creation

The joy of making something beautiful with your own hands — having worked through every challenge along the way to get there.

◈

Communication

Blowing glass well is a partnership. You practice asking for exactly what you need, clearly and right now — and it enhances relationships fast.

❋

Resilience

Glass breaks. The practice is learning how you react to it: the pivot, the acceptance, and the decision to start the next piece.

◐

Emotional regulation

A glowing gather asks for your whole, calm attention. For a while, the work pulls you fully into the present moment.

Sea-glass, amber, petrol — colors that mix like real stacked glass

Communication, at 1,900°F

Imagine you’re holding a rod with a 1,900° ball of molten glass on the end. You need your partner to hand you the right tool — immediately — or the glass drops and the piece is lost.

Glassblowing builds communication skills, quickly. We’re right there with you, coaching if you need it, as you practice asking for what you need in a clear, straightforward way.

And it gives your glass partner the chance to show up for you — meeting a simple, real need with their own fast response. That exchange, repeated at the bench, is a skill you carry back out the door.

Where we work

At home in the McGuffey Art Center.

We’re based at McGuffey, an artist-run cooperative that has been a home for Charlottesville makers since 1975. Our studio sits among working painters, potters, and printmakers — so every visit puts you inside a living creative community, not a clinic.
