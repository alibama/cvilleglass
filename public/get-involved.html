More than money

Ways to help right now.

Money matters, but it isn’t the only thing that gets a studio running. A few of these might be a better fit for you.

Volunteer

Help at open days, assist facilitators, or lend a professional skill — bookkeeping, grant writing, photography, web.

Partner with us

Clinicians, recovery programs, schools, and employers: let’s design sessions for the people you serve.

Refer someone

Know a person or group who’d benefit from studio time? Point them our way, or tell us about them.

Give in kind

Glass, tools, kiln and hot-shop equipment, safety gear, or studio materials — usable donations go straight to work.

Sponsor a seat

Underwrite pay-what-you-can seats so cost is never the reason someone stays home. Ask us how.

Spread the word

Follow along, share the site, and tell people what we’re building. Early word-of-mouth is worth a lot.

For considered giving

Donor-advised funds & planned gifts.

We’re establishing a donor-advised fund so supporters can give in a tax-smart, flexible way, and so larger or recurring gifts have a solid home. If you already give through a DAF, a family foundation, or a workplace match, we’d love to be on your list.

These arrangements are being finalized. Reach out and we’ll share the details as soon as they’re ready — and make sure your intentions are set up the way you want.
