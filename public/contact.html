Getting here

A block off the Downtown Mall.

McGuffey sits just north of Charlottesville’s Downtown Mall in a historic 1915 schoolhouse. There’s nearby street and garage parking, and the Mall’s shops and cafés are a short walk away — easy to make a visit into an afternoon.

Need step-free access or a quieter time to visit? Just say so when you write, and we’ll plan around it.

Who to reach

One inbox, real people.

For now, everything comes to hello@cvilleglass.org — programs, partnerships, volunteering, press, and giving. Tell us which it is in the subject line and we’ll route it to the right person.
