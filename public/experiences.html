How it works

Three simple steps.

1 · Give $75

Pick a flower or a bauble and give securely through Stripe. That donation reserves your experience.

2 · We schedule

We’ll email within a couple of days to find a time that works — evenings and weekends included.

3 · You make it

Come to the studio at McGuffey, make your piece with a maker beside you, and pick it up once it’s annealed.

Good to know

A few details.

What to wear. Closed-toe shoes and natural fibers (cotton) are safest near the furnace. Skip loose synthetics; tie back long hair. We provide glasses and any other safety gear.

Accessibility. The hot shop is on the lower level at McGuffey. Tell us what you need and we’ll make the session work for you — get in touch.

Scheduling & refunds. Because it’s a donation, it isn’t refundable, but we’ll always work with you to reschedule. Just let us know.
